# Blip

## Definition

A Blip is a minimal derived presence mark for a qualified Content or Aura
cluster at a representation tier where full Content detail is not useful. It
helps a person notice that spatially related work exists without opening a
surface or manufacturing a semantic record.

## Formation and interaction

The Field Ledger can qualify a Blip from field density, Aura overlap, and
spatial scale. A Blip is a projection of that evidence and has no independent
payload, Placement, Memory, or Anchor. Engaging it may open an Annotation or
focus the Grid, but engagement does not mutate the source.

## Boundary decisions

| Tension | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Blip vs Content | Blip summarizes Content evidence at a different representation tier. |
| Blip vs Aura | Blip is a visual interpretation of Aura/ledger evidence. |
| Blip vs Memory | Blip never creates or represents a new Memory record. |
| Blip vs Tier | Blip is a projection selected for a Tier; it is not a Tier itself. |
