AB-01 · ONE COMPLETE SHEET
MIXED MEDIA · NOTES ON 0102 / 23
01A sheet and its foldsA brochure is not a page with columns. This is the inside spread of a three-panel fold: the reader meets panels in sequence, then opens the sheet and sees them together. Six reading panels exist in all; three are on the reverse.
02The fold sets pacing, never type sizeA fold creates reveal and order. It is never a reason to shrink type to make material reach the next crease.
03An image may lead a panelThe frame is complete and proportional — never cropped to a slot, never enlarged past the point where it stays sharp. An image can lead a panel, share one with text, or occupy a reveal.
04A caption exists or it does notThis line prints because the source carries caption metadata. Where none exists, nothing appears, and a file name is never promoted into one.
05A title exists or it does notThe Document prints a title because the source carries title metadata. There is no generated title region anywhere on the sheet.
06Provenance travels with its sourceEvery frame keeps its own Layer label and placement count. Sources sharing a panel never merge into one voice.
07Untitled text starts at the measureThe third panel's Note carries no title, so it begins at the body measure. Its first sentence is not lifted into a headline.
08Empty space stays emptyThe lower half of the first panel holds nothing. No headline, caption, or label is invented to fill a panel.
09A panel is boundedA panel that cannot hold its text does not compress it: it creates another panel, or the set moves to the pamphlet form and paginates. AB-02.
10One sheet carries no page numberPage numbers are physical pagination. A single sheet has none, so the footer carries Layer context only and no pagination chrome appears. AB-01.

APF-01 · FACING LEAVES · 3:2 SPREAD
MIXED MEDIA · NOTES ON 0304 / 23
01Leaves, not panelsA pamphlet is a short sequence of leaves. One sheet folded once makes four pages; folios stack and stitch into a longer sequence. The spread reads near 3:2 overall while each leaf stays near portrait inside it.
02Two columns, one stable measureBoth leaves hold the same column width and the same explicit gutter. The measure does not change from leaf to leaf to make material fit.
03The binding stays quietNo essential sentence and no fragile image detail sits where the binding would take it. The inner margin is wider than the outer margin for that reason.
04Image and text stay pairedThe photograph and the Note that explains it share one leaf and one pair. Page assignment never separates them, and sources are never reordered to improve a pairing.
05The frame is completeThe image takes the column measure at its own proportion. It is not cropped to a square, and it is not enlarged to fill the leaf.
06Text continues at the same sizeThe Document runs from the first column into the second without a change of measure or type size. A column end is a break in the flow, not a break in the material.
07Continuation is statedThe last line of the leaf names the leaf that follows. The end of a leaf is a pagination boundary, never a truncation boundary. APF-02.
08Page numbers are physicalThe folio is a position in the sequence. It is never a source count, a breadcrumb, or a label like "Note 01". Layer context sits opposite it, on the outer edge.
09Two frames to a leafHolding a leaf to two frames keeps the pairing readable. A third source starts the next leaf; a set that keeps demanding leaves promotes to the magazine form. APF-02.

AMG-01 · PAGE 6 OF 9 · THREE THREADED COLUMNS
MIXED MEDIA · NOTES ON 0506 / 23
01Threaded text, three columnsThe Document is one flow poured through three column frames in order. Read end to end, the columns reconstruct the source exactly — nothing is dropped, reordered, or summarised on the way in.
02One baseline for the pageEvery column sits on the same line rhythm, so the three columns align across the page and across the fold. Line height is fixed; it is not adjusted per column to close a gap.
03Image modules span columnsA photograph takes whole column widths at its own proportion — here two of three. Modules are measured in columns so the page grid holds; they are never scaled to a leftover shape.
04Caption from metadata onlyThe caption prints because the source carries one. The line below it is the source's Layer and placement, which is provenance, not a caption.
05Continuation, not truncationThe thread reaches the end of the page and says where it resumes. Text that does not fit produces another page. AMG-01.
06Folio on every pagePage number on the outer edge, Layer context opposite. The count is physical pagination and never becomes a title or a source tally.
07Density becomes pagesMore material never becomes smaller type or a tighter measure. It becomes more pages — this is the mechanism that carries hundreds of sources without a wall of content.
08The binding edge is wideThis is the right half of a facing spread, so the inner margin is the wider one. Nothing essential is placed where the binding would damage or hide it.
09Three sources, three identitiesThree sources share this page and each keeps its own label, provenance, and route. A shared page never merges separate sources into one voice or one title.

B-01 … B-04 · FOLD CATALOGUE BELOW
MIXED MEDIA · NOTES ON 0708 / 23
01B-01 · single panelOne leaf, no fold. Used when the grouping is one short sequence that a single sheet can hold whole. An image may lead beside one readable column; nothing is added to make the sheet look busier.
02B-02 · tri-foldSix reading panels in a consecutive three-up arrangement — the two strips here are the outside and the inside of one sheet. A tri-fold is six bounded panels, never one page with three loose columns.
03B-03 · gatefoldTwo outer panels fold in to meet, then open to a wider centre. Six panels, with the reveal reserved for the image or relationship that earns the extra width. Solid creases fold inward.
04B-04 · accordionA concertina of six panels with creases alternating inward and outward, so the sequence has order without a single cover. Used when no one panel should lead.
05Half fold · four panelsOne crease. Two panels a side. The smallest fold that still produces a reading order rather than a single face.
06Tri fold · six panelsTwo creases in the same direction; panels stack inward. The physical count behind B-02.
07Gate fold · six panelsTwo creases folding inward with a wider centre. The count matches the tri-fold but the reading geometry does not, so it stays a separate template.
08Z fold · six panelsTwo creases in opposite directions. Same panel count as the tri-fold, zig-zag reveal order — again a different geometry, again a different template.
09Roll fold · eight panelsThree creases, each panel slightly narrower so it can roll inside the one before it. The widest capacity a brochure reaches before the set becomes a pamphlet.
A FOLD IS A READING GEOMETRY, NOT A DECORATION

P-01 … P-04 · LEAVES, NOT PANELS
MIXED MEDIA · NOTES ON 0910 / 23
01P-01 · text leafOne folio page, one column, no image. Used when text leads and no visual source is present. The absence of an image is not a hole to fill; no plate is invented for balance.
02P-02 · facing leavesAn illustrated folio: image on one leaf, the text it explains on the facing leaf. Used when proximity has already grouped a picture with its account of itself.
03Each leaf keeps its own footerFacing leaves are two pages, not one canvas. Each carries its own page number on the outer edge and its own Layer context — because their sources differ.
04P-03 · column leafA short article page: one leaf, two measures. Used when text demand needs a second column but not a second leaf. The measure is chosen once and holds for the whole sequence.
05The gutter is explicitColumn separation is a stated value, not whatever space happens to be left. The same gutter appears on every leaf of the sequence.
06P-04 · continuationA stitched sequence for one source that exceeds a leaf. One sheet folded once makes four pages; folios stack until the source is complete.
07An image may take its own leafWhen a picture needs more area than a shared leaf allows, it gets a plate leaf of its own rather than being reduced to fit beside the text.
08The sequence states where it goesThe final leaf names the leaf that continues it. A pamphlet may hold one source, several, or no image at all; the count changes pagination, never the form's name and never a generated title.
09Orientation follows the sourceA leaf is near portrait by default because that suits a column of text, but the composition of the sources decides. Portrait is a consequence, not a fixed shell.

M-01 … M-04 · PAGES AND SPREADS
MIXED MEDIA · NOTES ON 1112 / 23
01M-01 · image openerOne picture takes a full page. Chosen when a single image carries the group and has the resolution to hold the area — never to fill a page that text could not fill.
02An opener still carries its footerA dominant image does not suspend page context. Caption where metadata exists, provenance beneath it, page number and Layer at the foot.
03M-02 · mixed spreadImage and text hold distinct regions across facing pages. Chosen when both make a real claim on the page and neither should be reduced to an inset of the other.
04The binding is left clearNothing essential crosses the fold. A spread can lose content at the binding, so the region is treated as unusable rather than optimistic.
05M-03 · multi-image sequenceSeveral pictures paced across a spread at different sizes. Size follows each image's role and resolution, not a uniform tile — frames keep their own proportions.
06Deliberate asymmetry is allowedA spread may be unbalanced on purpose. What it may not do is crop a frame or invent a caption to make the two halves match.
07M-04 · long-run paginationThe threaded feature: one long text, or many sources, carried across an ordered run of pages. This is the capacity mechanism for hundreds of sources.
08The run states its continuationEach page ends by naming the next. Read in order, the pages reconstruct every source exactly once, complete and in source order.
09No issue title is inventedA long run has page numbers and Layer context and nothing else. It does not gain a masthead, an issue number, or a header synthesised from the source count.
A LONG RUN IS PAGINATION, NOT A PUBLICATION IDENTITY
MIXED MEDIA · MEASURING DEMAND13 / 23
Text demand · Atext
1Normalise rich text into readable blocks. Paragraph and list boundaries are kept; they are part of the demand.
2Render it at the minimum readable body measure — not at whatever measure the container happens to offer.
3Count the resulting lines, the line height, the paragraph spacing, and the space a continuation would need.
4Sum the occupied areas. One 500-word source outweighs five one-line notes, though both are five records.
charsPerLine = max(8, floor(measure / max(6, 0.52 × size))) lineCount = body lines + heading lines + blank units paragraphCost = 0.65 × paragraph breaks Tᵢ = max(0.25, (lineCount + paragraphCost) / linesPerPage) areaᵢ = (lineCount + paragraphCost) × size × leading × measure
Image demand · Aimage
1Read the pixel dimensions and native aspect ratio of every image. Both come from the source, never from a thumbnail on screen.
2Choose the smallest complete-frame rectangle that still preserves the image's role in the group.
3Cap that rectangle by effective resolution. Nothing is cropped or enlarged to fill a template slot.
4Sum the selected display areas. A GIF is measured exactly as an image; animation changes playback, not area.
pixelReserve = clamp(0.70, 1.50, 0.18 × √(w × h) / F) aspectReserve = clamp(0.00, 0.45, 0.12 × |ln r|) roleReserve = +0.35 hero · 0 supporting · −0.18 thumbnail Iᵢ = max(0.45, pixelReserve + aspectReserve + roleReserve) F = 96 thumbnail · 180 supporting · 320 hero
FIXTURE INPUTS, NOT ACCEPTED CONSTANTS: MEASURE 340PX · SIZE 16PX · LEADING 1.55 · 18 LINES PER PAGE. THEY MOVE WITH EVIDENCE, AND THE PERSON NEVER TUNES THEM.
NEVER A DEMAND INPUT: RAW CHARACTER COUNT · FILE BYTE SIZE · COMPRESSION · A THUMBNAIL'S CURRENT ON-SCREEN SIZE. AN UNAVAILABLE SOURCE KEEPS A RESERVED DEMAND AND ITS PROVENANCE — IT NEVER BECOMES ZERO.

Which vertical the shares choose
imageShare ≤ 0.25Text-led — Bulletin, Berliner, Broadsheet
0.25 < imageShare < 0.70Mixed-media — Brochure, Pamphlet, Magazine

Order of decision
source set → per-source demand records → D and shares → gate → vertical → tier → form → template
THE SOURCE NOTES' 60–70% MEDIA BAND IS A QUALIFICATION SIGNAL, NOT A TEMPLATE PICKER. A GROUP MAY STAY MIXED BELOW IT WHEN ITS TEXT AND IMAGES REMAIN INTERDEPENDENT. R MEDIA IS RE-READ LOCALLY FOR EACH PAGE TO CHOOSE A COMPOSITION — A PAGE-LEVEL RATIO NEVER RE-ROUTES THE WHOLE SET.
Measured again whenever
1A related Note, Document, Image, or GIF joins or leaves the set.
2Text is edited so wrapping, headings, or page count change.
3An image is replaced at a different resolution or aspect ratio.
4An image's role changes between hero, supporting, and thumbnail.
5A gate or tier threshold is crossed, in either direction.
6The usable surface changes class — portrait, near-square, landscape.
A PROMOTION ADDS PAGES OR CHANGES FORM. IT NEVER TRUNCATES, SUMMARISES, OR CREATES A MEMORY. HYSTERESIS HOLDS A SET SITTING ON A BOUNDARY SO THE READING CANNOT OSCILLATE, AND THE PERSON'S CURRENT SOURCE IS KEPT WHENEVER THE NEW SEQUENCE CAN CARRY IT. AP-04 · T-AN13.

01300 PPI and aboveThe image can hold the whole rectangle the group selected for it. It may take the full text measure, lead a panel, open a page, or occupy a reveal.
02Effective resolution decides, not file sizePlaced width and height against pixel width and height; the smaller of the two ratios wins. Compression bytes never enter it. Viewing distance is the production caveat, not a licence to enlarge.
03150 to 299 PPIThe frame stays bounded. It keeps its complete edges and its own proportion, but it does not take a dominant page and it is never given a full-bleed emphasis it cannot support.
04Bounded is not croppedThe middle band shows a smaller picture, not a trimmed one. Reducing the placed size raises effective resolution; cropping would lose the frame, which is never traded for area.
05Below 150 PPIA small supporting element, or nothing on the page at all. The source stays in its own viewer where it can be seen at its real size instead of being flattered by a page.
06The route stays openWhatever band an image falls in, the Image Viewer remains one explicit step away. A quality band changes how the page places a picture, never whether the person can reach it.
07Never upscaled to fillNo band permits enlargement past the point where the image goes soft. If a template slot is bigger than the picture can support, the template is wrong, not the picture. AMF-02.

Loaded Tuesday evening: twenty-two shelves of test tiles, the four porcelain beakers, and the two press-moulded dishes that have been waiting since March. Candled at 95°C for three hours because the beakers were thrown on Sunday and still felt cool against the back of the hand.

Ramp one held a hundred and thirty degrees an hour to 600°C without complaint. Front and back sat within four degrees of each other for the whole of it, which is better than this kiln usually manages and probably owes more to the weather than to anything done at the controller.

Ramp two ran faster than asked. The panel read two hundred and five degrees an hour where the schedule said one hundred and eighty, and by the time it was noticed the back of the chamber was already twelve degrees ahead of the front. The soak at the top was stretched to twenty minutes rather than fifteen to bring the two ends level again.

Cooling was slow on purpose: a crash to 900°C, a long hold there, then free fall with both dampers shut. Opened Thursday morning at 60°C.

AMF-00 … AMF-03 · FOUR SOURCES, FOUR IDENTITIES
MIXED MEDIA · NOTES ON 1819 / 23
01A title only where one existsThe Document carries title metadata, so its title prints. There is no generated title region on any page of any form.
02No title, no promotionThe Note has no title, so its text begins at the body measure. Its first sentence is not lifted, emboldened, or set larger to stand in for a headline.
03An unavailable source keeps its placeIt is named, kept in order, and its provenance and routes remain. It is never dropped from the page and never quietly counted as nothing. AMF-03.
04A caption only where one existsThe first picture carries caption metadata, so a caption prints beneath it.
05No caption, no substituteThe second picture has none, so no caption line appears. A file name is not a title and never becomes one.
06Provenance is per sourceEvery frame states its own Layer and how many placements it has. Four sources on one page keep four identities; they are never merged into a single voice or a single credit.
07The page number is a positionIt is physical pagination and nothing else — not a source count, not a breadcrumb, not a label like "Note 01". Layer context sits opposite it.
08Routes stay understatedEach source keeps its own way back — placement, the canonical record, and the surface that suits its form. They read as quiet type beneath the material, never as buttons competing with it.
09Zero notes is still a readingA cluster with no text at all keeps the image-only templates available; a cluster with hundreds becomes a page run. Neither one synthesises a heading from the count.

AP-01 → AP-02 → AP-03 · THE SAME SOURCES, MORE PAGES
MIXED MEDIA · NOTES ON 2021 / 23
01Light · one sheetThe set fits a single sheet whole, so it gets one. No pagination chrome appears, because there is no sequence to move through. AP-01.
02Growth promotes the formWhen the same sources grow past what a sheet can hold, the reading becomes a leaf sequence. The promotion happens before anything is compressed, never after. AB-02.
03Moderate · a spreadFacing leaves, each with its own folio, and a stated continuation. Source order is exactly the order it was in on the single sheet. AP-02.
04Growth promotes againPast the spread, the reading becomes an ordered run. Text keeps the same size and the same measure across every promotion; only the number of pages changes.
05Dense · a page runOrdered pages carry the material as far as it goes. This is the only capacity mechanism, and it scales to hundreds of sources without a change of grammar. AP-03.
06The run ends when the material doesThe last page names its continuation or it does not exist. There is no final page of padding and no cover invented for the sequence.
07The break at reading sizeHere is what a page boundary actually looks like: a sentence stops at the foot of one page and resumes at the head of the next, at the same size, in the same measure, mid-clause.
08Read in order, nothing is missingConcatenate the pages and the source returns exactly — every word, every frame, once each. That is the whole test for pagination. AMG-01.
09What is refused insteadShrinking type, tightening the measure, clipping a frame, hiding an item behind a scroll, or summarising. Each is available and each is forbidden; more pages is the answer every time.

KILNNOTE 01
×No shrinking to fitType never goes below the readable measure to save a page. More material means more pages — at every tier, on every surface.
×No cropping, no upscalingA frame keeps its complete edges and its own proportion. An image that cannot support an area gets a smaller one, never a trimmed one.
×No invented identityNo generated title, no headline lifted from a first sentence, no caption made from a file name, no page label like "Note 01".
×No empty template chromeA text-only group gets no empty image slot; a panel with nothing left to say stays empty rather than being filled with a label.
×No merged voicesSources sharing a page keep separate labels, provenance, and routes. Nothing is reordered, combined, or summarised to make a page compose better.
×No audio, video, PDF, or EPUBNotes, Documents, Images, and GIFs are the supported material. Unsupported forms never appear as a greyed option or a broken frame.
