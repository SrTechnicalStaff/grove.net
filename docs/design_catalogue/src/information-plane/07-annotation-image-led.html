1.0 ≤ D < 1.75 · ONE COMPLETE FRAME · LANDSCAPE SHEET
IMAGE LED · NOTES ON 0102 / 20
01One picture, wholeThe largest complete frame the source can carry. Nothing is trimmed to fill the sheet: if the picture will not fit, the frame gets smaller or another arrangement is chosen.
02The sheet yieldsThe frame follows the picture's own proportion and the page arranges itself around it. The reading unit stays landscape at every width.
03A label only if there is oneTitle and date appear because the source carries them. With no title, nothing is generated and no empty heading is held open.
04Facts sit outside the pictureDimensions, proportion, and date read in quiet mono beneath the frame, never printed over it. File size is absent: bytes say nothing about how much room a picture needs.
05Room to lookThe field around the frame is part of the form. It is inspection space for a picture that rewards close reading, not leftover area waiting to be filled.
06Where it came fromLayer and page run at the foot of every sheet, the way a magazine carries its folio. That line is provenance and pagination — never the picture's identity.

1.75 ≤ D < 3.0 · SEVERAL COMPLETE FRAMES · ONE COMPARISON SHEET
IMAGE LED · NOTES ON 0304 / 20
01Capture order holdsFrames read left to right, top to bottom, in the order the material came in. A sort by filename is convenience, not order, and never replaces it.
02One contact scaleFrames share a common height so they can be compared; widths follow each source. Identical rectangles are not the goal — comparison is.
03A wide source takes a wide frameA panorama gets the width it needs and its row simply holds fewer pictures. Alignment yields before a picture is distorted.
04Marks live in the marginFrame numbers and source facts sit outside the picture area, as they do on a proof sheet. Nothing is printed across an image.
05Selection never touches the pictureAn interaction-hue outline sits outside the frame. The image itself is never dimmed, tinted, or reframed to show that it is chosen.
06The sheet ends, it does not compressWhen the next frame will not fit at the contact scale, a second sheet begins. Nothing shrinks below the size at which it can still be compared.
07Sized for comparison, not for final viewingA sheet is a working record. A picture that needs real inspection is opened at full size rather than enlarged in place until it goes soft.

D ≥ 3.0 · FACING PAGES · LARGER FRAMES CONTINUE ACROSS THE TURN
IMAGE LED · NOTES ON 0506 / 20
01Two pages, one compositionFacing pages are composed together. What sits opposite a picture is a decision about reveal, pause, and contrast — not whatever was next in the list.
02The left page pausesOne picture at scale. Density is not the point here; the turn before and after it is part of the reading.
03Text supports and yieldsA short measure beside the picture stays complete and gives up width before the frame does. It never becomes the reason a frame is reduced.
04The gutter is not crossedNo picture is split across the fold and no caption reaches over it. Each page stands as a complete unit.
05Order continues through the turnPage numbers and picture order run on across pages, so the sequence survives pagination instead of restarting on each sheet.
06No slot waits for missing textWhere a source carries no caption, the space closes up. An empty title region is never reserved on the chance that text arrives.
07Layer and page at the footEach page carries where its material came from and where the reader is, in quiet mono, on both sides of the fold.

PAGINATED VISUAL SEQUENCE · I3-E
IMAGE LED · NOTES ON 0708 / 20
01Open with oneThe sequence starts with a single plate and a supporting page. The first turn establishes the pace the rest of the pages keep.
02A pair is a decisionTwo pictures face each other because proximity, sequence, contrast, or shared material makes the pairing mean something. Each stays independently inspectable.
03Varied scale is sequencingOne picture leads, one bridges, one closes. Deliberate variation is not a masonry wall: the sizes follow the order, not the packing.
04The set continuesMore material makes more pages. Nothing is reduced to a thumbnail so that a sequence can end on the current spread.
05Order carries meaningPicture numbers run across the turn and source boundaries survive. A stronger picture is not promoted to the opening unless the material supports that role.
06Promotion, and keeping your placeA plate becomes a sheet when comparison beats inspection; a sheet becomes a sequence when the pictures need readable scale and pacing. A smaller complete frame always beats a crop or an enlargement — and the picture being read stays the picture being read when pages are added.

EFFECTIVE PLACED RESOLUTION SETS THE CEILING ON DISPLAY AREA
IMAGE LED · NOTES ON 1213 / 20
01At or above the print referenceA clean source placed at 300 PPI or better can carry the display area it is given, including the leading frame on the sheet or a full-width moment in a sequence.
02Between 150 and 299A bounded frame. The picture is placed well inside what it can hold rather than pushed to the largest frame the page could offer.
03Below 150Small and supporting, or sent to full-size inspection on its own. A soft leading frame is never offered as a substitute for the source.
04The measure is placed resolutionEffective resolution is the source's pixels divided by the size it is actually placed at, taken on the tighter of the two dimensions. It falls as the frame grows, which is what makes it a ceiling.
05Inspection is a route, not a rescueWhen a source cannot carry a large frame, the reading offers the way to open it rather than enlarging it in place. The frame that stays on the page remains complete.
06Three bands route; they do not promiseSource dimensions, viewing distance, and the final medium remain the authority. The bands choose a frame — they are not a print-production guarantee, and each role also carries its own floor: 96, 180, 320.

FOUR FAMILIES · CLASSIFICATION SELECTS A FRAME, IT NEVER AUTHORISES A CROP
IMAGE LED · NOTES ON 1415 / 20
01Classify before composingEvery picture is landscape, portrait, square, or extreme panorama before any arrangement is chosen. Where a source does not match the region available, the region changes, the picture gets smaller, or another family is selected — in that order.
02LandscapeSits naturally inside the landscape unit with field around it. This is the default inspection frame and the reason the unit is landscape in the first place.
03PortraitTakes one side of the unit at full height, with support beside it. The unit is never swapped for a portrait page: a narrow width changes columns and page count instead.
04SquareEqual margins on all four sides. The page does not stretch it toward either edge to make a row look tidy.
05Extreme panoramaClaims the full width of a unit, or a whole spread when the detail needs it, and the rest of the set moves to the next page. Its row simply carries fewer pictures.

ANIMATION CHANGES PLAYBACK, NOT THE ROOM A PICTURE NEEDS
IMAGE LED · NOTES ON 1617 / 20
01A GIF is a picture that movesIts frame is complete and it asks for space by the same measure as a still: intrinsic dimensions, proportion, role, and fidelity floor. Animation changes playback, not area.
02Facts read the same wayKind, dimensions, and proportion sit under the frame in quiet mono, exactly as they do for a still. Nothing about the page announces that it is doing something special.
03Reduced motion loses nothingWith animation off, a complete static frame remains in the same place at the same size. Meaning never lives only in the movement.
04A still from a moving source is a derivativeIt may stand in only when it is identified as a frame taken from that source and the source itself stays reachable and named.
05Flattening is refusedPresenting moving material as a photograph is a lie about what the source is. Material of that kind gets playback or inspection; audio and video are not read here at all, and a source outside this material keeps its identity and its route instead of being turned into a picture.
