01Architectural sharpness2px is the entire corner language. Larger radii do not exist in Grove chrome; a pill or rounded card is a foreign object.
02Spacing has jobsGenerous separation between content and controls. Compact spacing inside a control group. Clear empty space around one primary action.
03Borders contain, shadows separateA 1px border establishes focus, containment, or refusal. A shadow only lifts a surface off the Grid — soft and subordinate, never a glow.

DESIGN LANGUAGE · REFUSALS06 / 06
×No scrim, no centered dialogNothing floats center-screen detached from what it serves. Confirmations are inline in the surface that asked.
×No truncation, no cropsContent is never shrunk, clipped, or ellipsized to fit a container. Collections keep complete frames; editions paginate.
×No card-ificationThe Grid is not a card layout. Not every surface is a card, a pill, or a tile; persistent chrome does not compensate for missing behavior.
×No invented accentsA screen with a new color is a screen that skipped the role table.
×No system vocabulary in copyOne architectural noun in user-visible copy fails review. A label names what a person can do — or says nothing.
×No meaning that lives only in motionThe settled frame carries the same information as the transition. Reduced motion swaps animation for instant state at identical thresholds.
