Walked the upper ridge at first light to see how the trail edge came through the winter. The soft ground begins a mile past the second gate and runs all the way to the overlook.

What the winter took

Two culverts are packed with leaf litter and want clearing before the rains come. The third held, and the stone apron below it is still sound. Above the switchback the bank has slipped by a foot or so — not urgent yet, but worth watching through the summer.

Photographs of each crossing are keyed to the map, so the crew can find them without me. Clear the culverts first; everything else can wait for dry weather.

01The whole text, alwaysThe slate holds the complete Document. Long text scrolls inside the surface; it is never clipped to a card-sized viewport and never faded out. WS-00 / WS-01.
02One fixed measureThe text column is ~65 characters, centred in the surface, and it does not change with the window. UI type at 16px on a 1.6 line — a page setting, not a panel setting.
03The emptiness is the designA form label, one way out, one footer line: that is the entire interface. The margin around the column is a stated value, not leftover room — nothing may be added to it.
04Metadata sits under the textForm and revision line in reduced-ink mono, aligned to the column and separated by a hairline. Mono supports the writing; it never sets it.
