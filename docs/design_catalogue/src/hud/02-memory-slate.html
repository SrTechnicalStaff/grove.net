Shoreline Erosion Survey
R. Okafor · Field Team North · April 2025
Seasonal observation of the north shoreline shows erosion concentrating at the tide line after the winter storms. We compare bank profiles from four field visits between November and March, and note where the planting held the slope and where it gave way under standing water. The two culverts below the ridge path carried more volume than the design allowed, and the softened bank above them accounts for most of the loss measured this season. Recommendations follow for the spring survey route, the order of repair, and the two crossings that should be watched through the next storm cycle.
