THE GRID STAYS FUNCTIONALLY ON — SNAPPING, PREVIEWS, SELECTION, CURSOR AND PRESENCE ARE PIXEL-IDENTICAL WITH LINES OFF.
01The lines are the only thing that leavesContent, presence, cursor and footprints are drawn from the same geometry either way. Nothing shifts by a pixel.
02The cursor still addresses cellsWith lines hidden the cursor keeps its whole-cell edges, so a person can still see exactly which cell they are on.
03A preference, not a modeHiding lines removes no behaviour and unlocks none. It is remembered for the person and applies wherever they are.

01Whole-cell addressingThe cursor is exactly one cell, edges on the grid's own lines: a 2px inset ring at 88% neutral over a 13% fill. Behind it, spent cells of its path decay to nothing.
02Selection, for contrastA selected placement carries the interaction outline #96B6F8 outside its frame, a soft glow, and presence in the cells around it. The cursor carries none of those — it points, it does not hold.
03It scales with the cellsThe cursor is measured in cells, never in screen pixels, so it shrinks with the field and stays on major boundaries at every zoom — including where lines are faded or hidden.
04Neutral until askedAt rest the cursor is 234 234 234. Placing, moving, resizing and tracing each recolour it; nothing else does.
