DISTANCE · THE WHOLE FIELD04 / 05
01Everything exists, everywhereAt 1% zoom every placement is still a mark at its true position, in its kind's hue. The census at any distance equals the census of the field — distance changes how much detail I see, never what exists.
02Terrain, not thumbnailsPresence fields read as lit-cell terrain under the marks. Cells keep hard edges at every scale; no smoothing replaces the cell, because the cell is the field's pixel.
03The camera is a lensOnly the top line level remains; finer levels faded as their spacing collapsed. The camera owns none of this — it is pure view state, and representation is derived from projected size alone.

DISTANCE · REFUSALS05 / 05
REFUSED · FIXED SCREEN SIZE
Nothing counter-scalesA placement's on-screen size is its footprint under the camera. Content pinned at a fixed screen size floats free of the field and lies about where — and how big — it is.
REFUSED · AGGREGATION
3
Distance never aggregatesThree placements are three marks at three positions. A cluster bubble replaces positions with a count, invents a shape the field does not have, and hides what exists.
REFUSED · EMPTY CELLS
Never an empty cellWhen no snapshot is ready the fallback is a kind-coded block — presence is unconditional. Unloading content for smoothness makes the Grid report less than it holds.
