01The outline sits outsideA 2px interaction outline runs clear of the content edge. The Note, the Document, the picture — none of them is restyled, tinted, or dimmed to say it is selected.
02The field answersThe cells around each selection brighten, hard-edged like every other cell value. Outline, field, and accent carry the state together, so it survives without colour.
03Many at once, one grammarEvery selected placement wears the same outline and the same field response, whatever form it holds. No badges, no counts riding on the content.
04Never a property of contentUnselected placements are untouched. Selection lasts as long as the work does — it is never saved with a Note, a Document, or a picture.

01The whole footprint, before commitThe preview covers every cell the content will occupy — complete cells, at the pointer, at the current scale. Size is stated in the corner, quietly.
02Never mistakable for contentA dashed edge and a transparent fill. The preview carries no text, no surface treatment, and no weight of its own: it is a promise about cells, not a thing on the Grid.
03The source keeps its placeThe origin is marked and left alone. Nothing durable moves while the gesture is open, so letting go early costs nothing.
04Validity is liveThe preview answers on every cell it crosses, so the answer arrives while the hand is still moving. A refusal is never a surprise at the end.

01Invalid names the refusalThe preview edge turns to the invalid role the moment the footprint stops fitting. One hue, one meaning, used nowhere else.
02Structure says it tooThe cells that cannot take the content are hatched. Read with no colour at all, the blocked cells still read as blocked.
