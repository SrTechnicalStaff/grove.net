01Mono · 11 · 0.20em · 48%Front matter. Uppercase and tracking are a label format, so it reads as credit, not as writing.
02Display · 38 / 0.98The title, and the only display type on the page. Tight leading keeps two lines reading as one block.
03UI · 15 / 1.65 · 34chThe abstract. Measure is set in characters, not in page width, so line length stays comfortable at any footprint.

01WorkingThe whole front page — front matter, title, abstract, close, title block — at its true place and extent, with presence in the cells around it.
02Stepped backStill the true page, still every word: only the inset edge and the paper texture step away. Nothing is replaced by a summary or a label.
03FarBelow 18px the page becomes a sheet stand-in on the exact 2×2 footprint: paper, a ruled head, four lines. Enough to read as a page, and never an empty cell.
04Thresholds carry hysteresisDemote at 56px, promote back at 72px; stand-in below 18px, page back above 28px. The gap is what stops a placement flickering while the view drifts.
05Promotion in placeComing back, the stand-in is replaced where it already sits. A page still resolving shows the stand-in — never a blank, never a spinner.
