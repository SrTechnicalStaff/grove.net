# Product Outcome: Preserve Context Through Anchoring

> **Plain-English Human-Behavior Statement**:
> "I can label and connect my thoughts to specific places and surrounding ideas, so my context is preserved naturally rather than requiring me to memorize rigid folder structures."

---

## 1. Behavior Change Description

In traditional digital tools, people are forced to translate organic human thought into rigid hierarchical folder trees, tag taxonomies, or explicit database categories. Over time, the context in which an idea was formed is lost because the system forces the person to remember where an item was stored or what label was assigned to it.

With context preservation through anchoring:
- The person places a piece of information next to related work on an open surface.
- Labels and notes attach directly to specific locations and surrounding materials.
- Cognitive context is retained passively: returning to a physical arrangement immediately recalls why items belong together without needing manual categorization or mental recall.

---

## 2. Real-World Human Impact

- **Before**: A researcher finds a valuable quote, copies it into a nested document folder, and weeks later cannot remember why the quote mattered or what project phase it belonged to.
- **After**: The researcher places the quote directly beside the diagram and note that inspired it. The surrounding arrangement acts as a visual anchor that instantly communicates meaning to a complete stranger or to the researcher months later.

---

## 3. Product Architecture Mapping

- **Core Primitives**: `Memory`, `Grid`, `Content`.
- **System Mechanics**:
  - **Content Tracing**: A Memory can have multiple Content instances across Grid Layers without duplicating the semantic record. Each Content instance may carry its own authored Anchor context.
  - **Spatial Proximity**: Distance on the 2D plane replaces folder containment as the primary carrier of meaning.
  - **Zero-Structure Overhead**: No mandatory tags, categories, or folder trees required to establish relationships.
