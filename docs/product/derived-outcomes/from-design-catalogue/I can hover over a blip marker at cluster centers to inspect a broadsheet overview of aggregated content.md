# I can hover over a blip marker at cluster centers to inspect a broadsheet overview of aggregated content

## Outcome
> I can hover over a blip marker at cluster centers to inspect a broadsheet overview of aggregated content.

## Behavior Scenarios

### Blip Hover Escalation
Hovering over a 14px blip marker at a distance promotes the marker into a full broadsheet annotation overlay detailing cluster information.
