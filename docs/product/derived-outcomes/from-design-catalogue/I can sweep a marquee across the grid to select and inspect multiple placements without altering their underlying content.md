# I can sweep a marquee across the grid to select and inspect multiple placements without altering their underlying content

## Outcome
> I can sweep a marquee across the grid to select and inspect multiple placements without altering their underlying content.

## Behavior Scenarios

### Non-Destructive Marquee Selection Sweep
Dragging across open cells renders a dashed marquee box in `--signal-active-work` (`#E8B964`); all intersecting notes highlight with selection outlines.
