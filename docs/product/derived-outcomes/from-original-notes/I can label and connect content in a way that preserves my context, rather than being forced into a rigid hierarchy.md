# I can label and connect content in a way that preserves my context, rather than being forced into a rigid hierarchy

## Outcome
> I can label and connect content in a way that preserves my context, rather than being forced into a rigid hierarchy.

## Behavior Scenarios

### Context Preserved Across Placements
Given content placed on the Grid, when I anchor a label or note to it, the relationship is established in-place at the exact grid coordinates, keeping my visual context intact.

### Non-Destructive Association
When I re-locate or re-orient anchored items, the underlying association persists without requiring folder re-indexing or manual key updates.
