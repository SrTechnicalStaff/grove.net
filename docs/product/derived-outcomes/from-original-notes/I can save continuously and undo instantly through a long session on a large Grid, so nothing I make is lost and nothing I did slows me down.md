# I can save continuously and undo instantly through a long session on a large Grid, so nothing I make is lost and nothing I did slows me down

## Outcome
> I can save continuously and undo instantly through a long session on a large Grid, so nothing I make is lost and nothing I did slows me down.

## Behavior Scenarios

### Invisible Persistence
Given a long working session with continuous edits and moves, changes commit to disk in the background without modal save dialogs or interaction pauses.

### Instant Undo/Redo
When I undo or redo operations repeatedly across hundreds of steps, each step applies immediately regardless of total grid size.
