# I can edit and arrange what is in front of me with immediate response no matter how much exists elsewhere, so the piece I am touching never pays for the size of the whole

## Outcome
> I can edit and arrange what is in front of me with immediate response no matter how much exists elsewhere, so the piece I am touching never pays for the size of the whole.

## Behavior Scenarios

### Typing Stays Immediate
Given a vault holding tens of thousands of placements, when I type into a Note or Document on the Grid, every keystroke renders immediately with zero latency.

### Dragging Stays Attached
Given hundreds of placements on the current Layer, when I drag a selection across the grid, the moved content stays strictly attached to my pointer without micro-stutter or frame drops.
