# Product Outcome: Effortless Broad-Overview Synthesis

> **Plain-English Human-Behavior Statement**:
> "I can instantly inspect an organized broadsheet summary of related information gathered across multiple layers without opening individual files or zooming around."

---

## 1. Behavior Change Description

When trying to understand a large cluster of research notes, documents, and images created over time, people have to manually click into dozens of separate items, read each one individually, and build a mental summary. This process is time-consuming and error-prone.

With effortless broad-overview synthesis:
- Proximity to a dense work cluster presents a minimal blip marker anchored to the cluster center.
- Selecting the marker opens a broadsheet annotation overlay (brochure, pamphlet, or magazine layout) that aggregates text and media from all visible Grid Layers into a clean print layout.
- The person gets a comprehensive, formatted overview without destroying spatial canvas context.

---

## 2. Real-World Human Impact

- **Before**: An investigator reviewing 30 notes and pictures scattered across four project layers must open each file one by one to compile an executive summary.
- **After**: The investigator hovers over the cluster blip and opens a multi-page pamphlet annotation. The system presents all 30 items cleanly organized into chapters with layer attribution footers.

---

## 3. Product Architecture Mapping

- **Core Primitives**: `Annotation`, `Blip`, `Aura`, `Content`.
- **System Mechanics**:
  - **Cluster Quantification**: Field ledger queries determine candidate source sets and media-to-text demand ratios.
  - **Broadsheet Template Matrix**: Automatically routes content into brochure, pamphlet, or magazine templates without text truncation.
  - **Source Routing**: Every item in the summary provides a direct route back to its native spatial location.
