Grove - Field ledger
The Field ledger pertains to aura fields, aura fields are the property that all content holds on the grid. 
Field ledger is the method in which relationships are stored as information.
On the grid, every cell has an innate value related to information. This value is at a default that is non-zero, because the simple existence of the cell itself is also valuable information. Relationships between content are innate, simply being on the grid establishes a piece of content's bearing on all other content. In this way we acknowledge how the grid behaves like space time, and the visualization of fields mirrors that of a weak force, such as gravity.
